# V22 — Market Microstructure

The selected-market visualization is refined as a market microstructure view.

- Sellers and buyers remain persistent actors.
- The shared order book remains the center of the visualization.
- Best ask, spread, fundamental, best bid, and trade count are shown as a compact state strip.
- A trade tape exposes recent recorded matches at the selected event.
- All values are derived from archived observations; no simulated events are added by the UI.
- Campaign view remains separate: 84 cells are independent market sessions.

The visual hierarchy is Campaign → Market → Event → Order/Trade → Allocation efficiency.
