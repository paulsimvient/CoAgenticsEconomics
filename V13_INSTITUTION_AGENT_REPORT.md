## Interpretation boundary
The ZI-C chamber is the institutional floor. Agent delta is descriptive: policy efficiency minus the matched institutional baseline. Intervention delta is treated minus control. The ten model IDs are qualification identities using controlled mechanisms, not ten live LLM calls; live-model claims require provider executions through the v8 harness.

High efficiency alone is not attributed to model intelligence. The ZI-C floor estimates what the institution can generate without learning or strategic reasoning; departures are then tested under matched interventions.
